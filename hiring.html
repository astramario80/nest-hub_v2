Applications & interviews

Rate each criterion from 1 — developing, 2 — capable with support, 3 — ready independently, to 4 — ready to lead others. Consider role knowledge, clear communication, problem-solving, and teamwork. Total: up to 16; a partial total shows how many criteria have been rated.

Interview scoring guide

Role knowledge: understands the position’s responsibilities and gives relevant examples. Communication: listens, explains ideas clearly, and responds to questions. Problem-solving: identifies a practical approach and explains the reasoning. Teamwork: supports others, shares responsibility, and handles disagreement constructively.
