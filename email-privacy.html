NEST™ Gmail email privacy

Updated October 4, 2026. This notice explains how the Gmail connection in the NEST™ group email window uses your Google account information.

Connecting your account

Google verifies your email address and asks you to allow NEST™ to send email from your account. NEST™ requests your email address and permission to send messages. It does not request permission to read your Gmail inbox.

Your connected email address appears in the composer so you can check the sending account. Google connection credentials stay in memory for the current page visit and expire automatically. They are cleared when you disconnect, sign out of NEST™, lose access or leave the page.

Sending a message

When you click Send email, NEST™ checks your current leadership access and selected division recipients. Your selected recipients, subject, message and NEST™ banner are then sent directly to Google Gmail to deliver the email from your connected account. Recipients can see the sender and the other addresses in the To field.

The email window keeps your draft while it is open. NEST™ does not store copies of Gmail messages, drafts or Gmail connection credentials on its server. Google stores and delivers sent messages through your Gmail account under your Google or school account policies.

Google account information obtained by this feature is used to identify the sending account and send the messages you choose. It is not used for advertising or sold to third parties.

Your choices

Connecting Gmail does not send a message. You review the recipients and message and choose when to send. Disconnect Gmail clears the current connection in NEST™. To remove Google's ongoing permission, visit your Google account connections and remove NEST™.

For questions about this feature, contact Mario Penalver. School account access remains subject to your district's Google settings.
