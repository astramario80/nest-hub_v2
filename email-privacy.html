NEST™ Communications email privacy

Updated October 22, 2026. NEST™ Communications lets current division leaders compose and submit team messages inside NEST using their existing NEST sign-in.

Sending and replies

Messages are sent from NEST Communications at communications@gknest.org through Postmark. NEST does not connect to your Gmail account or request permission to read or send through your Gmail inbox. Your verified NEST account or linked school address supplies the reply address; recipients can reply there using their usual email application.

Before each submission, NEST checks your current leadership permissions and selected recipients against current school records. Each recipient receives a separate email, with the subject, formatted message and NEST banner. Other recipients’ addresses are not included in that email.

Data handling

Your draft stays in the open composition window. NEST sends the selected addresses, reply address, subject and message to Postmark for delivery. The Postmark server credential stays on the server and is never shared with managers’ browsers.

NEST keeps hashed identifiers, request fingerprints, send states and counts for up to seven days to prevent duplicate sends. This record does not contain message text or recipient addresses. Separate NEST access diagnostics record limited status and timing information. Postmark processes and retains delivery activity and message contents under the account’s retention settings, currently the default 45 days. NEST enables open tracking for new messages and keeps link tracking disabled. Postmark uses a tracking image to observe opens and processes associated technical information. NEST displays only the recipient address and first observed open time; it does not return location, IP address or device details to student senders.

Sent history and open activity

Signed-in students can view only the messages sent through their own NEST account, including the original message content, recipient addresses and observed open activity. This history is retrieved from Postmark and is available for the account’s current 45-day retention window. Earlier messages sent through Gmail are not imported.

Open rates are approximate. Some email clients block tracking images or load them automatically. An observed open does not prove that a person read the message; no detected open does not prove that they did not read it. Viewing the message in NEST history blocks remote images, so the preview does not create tracking opens.

Delivery and choices

“Accepted” means Postmark accepted a message for delivery; it does not guarantee that it reached the inbox. NEST does not automatically resend messages after uncertain or partial results. Contact a NEST administrator to check delivery before sending again.

Announcement messages include a Postmark unsubscribe link. Replies go to your linked email inbox; NEST does not provide an inbox or read replies. For questions about this feature, contact Mario Penalver.
