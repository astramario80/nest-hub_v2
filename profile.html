Staff access & division delegation

Students receive basic access to their roster periods when they register; their current student leadership assignments still apply. Any Bethel staff member can create an account using their verified @bethelsd.org email. Staff start with basic access until you delegate divisions.

Viewer: the same division data and tools as a student without a leadership role. Manager: that division’s manager tools and data. Website admin: access across all divisions and website teaching tools. Account credentials, staff delegation, and diagnostics remain owner-only.

Division access

Choose Viewer or Manager for each assigned division. Leave other divisions at No access.

You can set access before registration. The staff person creates their own username and password through Create account. Saving a change signs them out everywhere.

Active account directory

All active registered student and staff accounts, sorted by last name, first name. Created times use Pacific time in YYYY-MM-DD-HH:mm:ss format. This shows enabled accounts, not users currently online.

Passwords cannot be displayed. Stored password hashes and salts are available in each account’s details. Notes are visible only to NEST administrators. Editing a manual account’s credentials or removing its access signs it out everywhere.

Help with signup attempts

Give one recognized student or staff email a fresh signup attempt. This clears its email cooldown and existing verification-code attempt count, and allows it past the shared-network limit for 10 minutes. Email verification is still required. No email is sent by this button.

Create a student account

Choose a period and a student from the live roster. Their district email and current leadership role determine their NEST access. Give the generated username and password to the student directly. A recovery address is optional; it must be an approved district address, and you may use your own.
